Lemon Garlic Pasta

Serves 4 · 25 minutes

Boil 400 g spaghetti in well-salted water. Meanwhile, warm olive oil with four sliced garlic cloves until fragrant, then add the zest and juice of two lemons. Toss with the pasta, a ladle of cooking water and plenty of parmesan.

Ingredients

  • 400 g spaghetti
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 lemons
  • 60 g parmesan
  • Olive oil, salt, pepper
